Yoga Course for Beginners in India
Shiva Yoga Niketan has developed a new yoga system for newcomers. The four-week preparatory yoga series introduces students to all the basic fundamentals of Yoga. In most of the present time, there is a dilemma in most of all, whether yoga knowledge will be helpful for us? Will we be able to do meditation, asanas and postures correctly in the difficult yoga techniques? Will we understand the changes within the body which is happening with yoga? Keeping in mind the questions of students, Shiva Yoga Niketan has started a special yoga series to develop the deep yoga understanding among all. In this yoga series, students will be told how they can understand the science of life through yoga power and fulfil their living values so that they can improve their life in physical, moral and social manner.
What you will learn?
During the course with the help of asanas and different body postures student will be told about the problems of the human body and their body abilities for better yoga performance.
With the help of right asana and postures, students will be able to understand how they can correct their shortcomings and do the things with stability.
By passive Stretching techniques Students will feel the energy and flexibility in their health. By applying some simple asana, students will be able to understand and co-ordinate with their architecture of nervous system.
With the help of regular pranayama, Students will experience energy in himself. At the same time, students will be able to know the reasons for obstructs in the body’s energy. With the help of Pranayama, the causes of obstruction of physical energy can also be treated.
Meditation power will be developed from regular yoga practice, as it is necessary today for everyone. Also, Students will be given best advices on yoga practice as well as proper guidance in order to strengthen their body health.
With the help of philosophical and moral knowledge, students will develop awareness towards different dimensions of life so that they can experience inner peace and fulfilment from within.
All the teachers of Shiva Yoga Niketan will interact with each student personally and give proper guidance to the students based on their health so that they can give their life a better change. The initial yoga series will prove to be useful for all students.



In this Yoga series of Shiva Yoga Niketan, you will be able to understand the history, yoga philosophy and development of yoga, as well as you will analyze all the famous yoga literature in depth. You will know what is the goal of yoga and what kind of yoga practice can you do to fulfill those goals. This yoga series will introduce you to the purpose of yoga so that you can use yoga knowledge to improve your personal and professional life.
